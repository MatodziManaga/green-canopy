ABOUT US

Rooted in community. Focused on the future.

We believe environmental change becomes meaningful when communities have the opportunity to lead it.

OUR STORY

Starting with a simple idea: grow where we live.

The Green Canopy Initiative is a community-focused environmental organisation based in Johannesburg.

Our work centres on urban greening and tree planting in schools, public spaces and communities across Gauteng. We aim to make environmental participation practical, inclusive and locally relevant.

We see tree planting as more than an environmental activity. It can create opportunities for education, collaboration, volunteering and community ownership.
