OUR PURPOSE

Every tree is a step towards a healthier community.

The Green Canopy Initiative is focused on practical, community-led environmental action.

We believe that meaningful climate action can begin in the places where people live, learn and work. By supporting tree planting and urban greening, we help communities create spaces that are more welcoming, resilient and connected to nature.
