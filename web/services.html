OUR PROGRAMMES

Where we focus our work

Our projects are built around three connected areas of action.

01 · TREE PLANTING

Greener public spaces

We support tree planting activities that help introduce more greenery into community spaces, schools and neighbourhoods.

  • Community planting days
  • Indigenous tree planting
  • Tree care and maintenance awareness
02 · SCHOOL GREENING

Learning through nature

We help schools incorporate environmental action into their communities and learning environments.

  • School tree planting
  • Environmental awareness activities
  • Learner participation
03 · COMMUNITY ACTION

People powering change

We create opportunities for volunteers, community groups and organisations to participate in environmental projects.

  • Volunteer opportunities
  • Community partnerships
  • Environmental events
